SparkTread is a single-player pixel-art tank battle for iPhone — the arcade tank duels that used to eat our coins, rebuilt from scratch for touch.

Your base sits at the bottom of the arena. Enemy tanks roll in from the top. Hold the line.

TWELVE STAGES, FOUR WORLDS
Fight through the frontier, the floodplain, the frozen works and the iron citadel. Every world changes the ground under your treads, and every stage brings its own enemy roster.

TWENTY ENEMY TANK TYPES
Quick scouts, heavy gunners, shielded armor — each type carries its own weapon and its own habits. By the final stages you meet all of them at once.

FIVE WEAPON FAMILIES
Normal, rapid, flame, armor-piercing and explosive rounds. Pick-ups swap your gun mid-fight, and each family reads differently against the four wall materials: steel only yields to armor-piercing fire, and polished steel yields to nothing.

THE TERRAIN IS A TACTIC
Blast brick open to make a lane, cross water with an amphibious kit, fit anti-skid treads for ice, and set the grass alight to clear a line of sight.

PICK-UPS THAT TURN A FIGHT
Speed, armor and firepower upgrades, an extra life, a base shield, an enemy freeze, a screen-clearing bomb — and bricks knocked loose from the walls, which rebuild the one around your base.

THREE DIFFICULTIES
Casual, Standard and Veteran. Casual gives you five lives, slower enemy decisions and a generous flow of pick-ups. Veteran gives you one life and enemies that shoot like they mean it.

ALSO INSIDE
• Progress saved stage by stage — put the phone down mid-campaign, pick it up later
• A Training Arena to learn the weapons and the terrain without spending lives
• Six languages, following your phone's setting
• High-contrast mode, a larger HUD, reduced motion, haptics, mirrored controls
• Plays entirely offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, single player only.

Landscape, iPhone, two thumbs.
