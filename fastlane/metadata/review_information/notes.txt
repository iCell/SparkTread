SparkTread is a single-player, offline arcade game. No account, no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content — so no demo credentials are needed and nothing is gated.

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
An original, modern take on the top-down tank-defence arcade format: the player drives one tank in a walled arena, destroys the enemy tanks rolling in, and keeps them from reaching the base at the bottom. It is entertainment rather than a utility. The gap it fills is that this widely remembered arcade format is served on iPhone mostly by ad-driven clones and emulator-style on-screen D-pads; SparkTread is built for touch from the ground up, with a twelve-stage campaign, three difficulties, no advertising and no in-app purchases. Audience: players aged 9 and up who enjoy short-session retro arcade games. Rated 9+ for frequent cartoon violence — pixel-art tanks firing at pixel-art tanks, no blood, no human figures.

HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
Landscape only. Launch, then on the title screen tap Start Game, choose Casual, Standard or Veteran, and stage 1 begins after a stage card. The left thumb is a four-way movement pad; the right thumb fires. Destroy every enemy tank while keeping your base (the eagle at the bottom) intact and your lives above zero. Stages unlock in sequence; progress saves automatically, and Continue resumes it. The title screen also has a Training Arena (free play, no lives lost) and Settings: sound volume, haptics, language (English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish — the device language by default), mirrored controls, high contrast, larger HUD, reduced motion, and a Feedback button that opens the reviewer's own mail app with a prefilled diagnostics footer, which sends nothing unless the reviewer sends it. Fastest path: Casual gives five lives and the slowest enemies, so a stage can be cleared in about two minutes.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) is the only external service the app contacts. It receives aggregate gameplay events — stage started, cleared, failed, campaign completed — with the stage number and id, difficulty, score and lives, plus Firebase's standard technical context. There is no IDFA and no ad network, no tracking across apps or websites, no third-party login, no payment processor, no AI service, no external data provider and no server operated by the developer. Simulation, rendering, audio, input and saved progress all run on-device with Apple frameworks. The game is fully playable in Airplane Mode.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Identical features and content in every region. The only region-dependent behaviour is the interface language, which follows the device setting and can be overridden in Settings. No region-locked content, no regional servers, no geographically gated features.

REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Neither applies. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material. Everything in it is the developer's own work: the source code, the pixel art drawn for this app, the stage layouts, and the audio, which is synthesized by a generator committed in the project's repository with a single cue supplied by the developer. No third-party brand, logo, trademark, recording or licensed asset appears in the app, and no third-party content is displayed at runtime.
