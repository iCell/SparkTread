# ADR-0029: No Third-Party Audio Ships

Status: Accepted by the owner on 2026-10-10, in answer to item 6 of App Review's information request for 0.1.0 ("先换成合成音效，再回复"). Supersedes the rights decision in ADR-0011 and the standing rule that the stage end is the reference's own results excerpt.

## Context

ADR-0011 (2026-09-10) recorded the owner's decision that the game's sounds were to be the original 决战坦克 sounds: wherever the public gameplay recording BV14b411K7bv held an isolated instance of a sound, an excerpt of that recording was bundled (attribution `excerpt`), and the rest was synthesized from measurements. `Tools/audio_manifest.json` recorded the rights position plainly — *"no third-party licence is held — the decision and its responsibility are the owner's"*.

Six shipped cues were such excerpts: `sfx_fire_normal`, `sfx_hit_steel`, `sfx_pickup_collect`, `sfx_player_explode`, `sfx_stage_win`, `sfx_tank_explode`. A seventh, `sfx_fire_rapid`, was that shot replayed 12 % faster — a derivative of one of them.

On 2026-10-10 App Review replied to the 0.1.0 submission with a standard information request for a developer account with limited review history. Its item 6 asks the developer to *"provide any relevant documentation or credentials to demonstrate you are authorized to provide … protected third-party material"*. Answering that the app contains none would contradict the repository's own manifest; answering truthfully would tell App Review the app ships unlicensed third-party audio.

## Decision

**No audio taken from a recording of another game ships in the product.** The seven cues are synthesized by `Tools/build_audio_assets.py` from the same measurements the rest of the set already used, and the answer to item 6 becomes that the app holds no third-party material at all.

What is kept and what is not:

- **Kept: the measurements.** Sweep endpoints, band balances, envelope shapes, stroke spacing and levels stay, exactly as the pixel art keeps what reverse-analysis taught it. What a sound *does* is not the recording.
- **Kept: every cue's function, duration and level.** Each replacement lands at the duration and the RMS the 2026-10-01 mix pass gave its predecessor, so no cue moved in the mix and §12.4's priorities still hold. Verified by measurement, not by ear alone.
- **Kept: one stage-end cue for both outcomes.** The owner settled on 2026-09-10 that winning and losing a stage end with the same sound, and that is unchanged. Only its material changes.
- **Not kept: the excerpts, and the extractor's place in the build.** `Tools/extract_reference_audio.py` stays in the repository as history; it is no longer a step in producing anything that ships, and `Scripts/check-audio.sh` no longer requires its hash.
- **Unaffected: `sfx_stage_card`.** It is the owner's own audio, generated by the owner and supplied on 2026-10-01, not an extract of anything. It remains the one cue the generator refuses to write.

The stage end is now a synthesized drum riff built to the measurement that had been kept in the generator for exactly this purpose: the owner's "dong ×6, ×6, ×1, ×4" phrasing, strokes ≈0.12 s apart inside a group and ≈0.24 s between groups, each stroke's body 150–195 Hz, broadband, ≈120 ms to −10 dB. A kit and a phrasing are reproduced; no tune is. This is the amendment to the standing rule in ADR-0011 — "the stage-end sound is the 决战坦克 results excerpt for both outcomes; never reintroduce a synthesized stage-end stinger" — and the protection on the name `sfx_stage_win` is lifted with it.

## Consequences

- The binary changes, so 0.1.0 (1) cannot be the submitted build: a new build is archived and uploaded, and the reply to App Review goes out against it.
- `Tools/audio_manifest.json` records the new position in `creative_decision` and `rights_review`; its `extractor` fields are gone, and no entry carries attribution `excerpt`.
- The audio the owner has been playtesting since September changes in seven places. The levels do not, but the character does, and the owner has not auditioned the replacements — that audition is the one piece of this decision still outstanding. §17 of `GAME_RULES.md` is not involved; this is not a balance change.
- Git history still contains the excerpt bytes. Removing them from history is a separate decision and was not taken here.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Keep the excerpts and disclose them to App Review.** Honest, but it tells Apple in writing that the app ships unlicensed third-party audio, and invites both rejection and a complaint from the rights holder.
- **Seek a licence.** The owner did not pursue it; the holder of a 1990s arcade title's sound is not readily reachable and the review is waiting.
