SparkTread — Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

SparkTread is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that your game talks to. We do not sell data, and the app shows no advertising.

What the app collects

Gameplay analytics. The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how far players get and where they get stuck. It records events named stage_start, stage_clear, stage_fail and campaign_complete, with the stage number and id, the chosen difficulty, the score and the lives remaining, plus three summary properties: the furthest stage cleared, the number of stages cleared, and the difficulty last played. Firebase adds its own standard technical context: a random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate region.

This data is not tied to your name, your email, your Apple Account or your contacts. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not ask to track you, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Nothing else. The app does not use the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, precise location, health data, or the network for anything other than the analytics described above.

Data that stays on your device

Your campaign progress, high scores and settings are stored on your iPhone. They are included in your own device or iCloud backups if you have backups turned on, and they are removed when you delete the app. They are not uploaded to us.

Feedback email

Settings has a Feedback button. Tapping it opens your own mail app with a message addressed to the developer and a short diagnostics footer already written in it: app version and build, system version, device model, language, current difficulty and stages cleared. You see all of it before sending, you can edit or delete any of it, and nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do write, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote, and we keep it only as long as needed to answer you.

Children

SparkTread is rated 9+ and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.
  • To ask what analytics data exists for your app installation, or to ask for it to be deleted, write to the address below. Because the analytics identifier is random and not linked to you, we may need the approximate dates and device you played on to find it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new date above.

SparkTread — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 9 日

SparkTread 是一款完全在你设备上运行的单机游戏。它没有账号、不需要登录，游戏过程也不与我们的任何服务器通信。我们不出售数据，应用内也没有广告。

应用收集什么

游戏数据统计。应用使用 Google Firebase Analytics，用于在汇总层面了解玩家能打到哪里、卡在哪里。记录的事件为 stage_start、stage_clear、stage_fail、campaign_complete，附带关卡序号与 ID、所选难度、分数和剩余生命，以及三项汇总属性：已通过的最远关卡、已通关数量、最近一次所选难度。Firebase 另会附加其标准技术信息：一个随机的应用实例标识符、设备型号、系统版本、应用版本、语言和大致地区。

这些数据不与你的姓名、邮箱、Apple 账户或通讯录关联。应用不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不会请求追踪权限，也不会跨其他应用或网站追踪你。

除此之外没有别的。应用不使用相机、麦克风、相册、通讯录、精确位置或健康数据；除上述统计外，不进行任何网络通信。

留在你设备上的数据

战役进度、分数和设置保存在你的 iPhone 上。如果你开启了备份，它们会包含在你自己的设备或 iCloud 备份中；删除应用即一并清除。这些数据不会上传给我们。

意见反馈邮件

设置里有"意见反馈"。点击后会打开你自己的邮件应用，收件人是开发者，正文已写好一段简短的诊断信息：应用版本与构建号、系统版本、设备型号、语言、当前难度和已通关数。发送前这些内容你都看得到，可以修改或删除；除非你按下发送，什么都不会寄出。如果你写信给我们，我们会看到你的邮箱地址和信件内容，并仅在答复所需的期间保留。

儿童

SparkTread 的年龄分级为 9+，并非面向 13 岁以下儿童设计。它不收集任何人的个人信息，儿童亦然。

你的选择

  • 删除应用即清除设备上保存的全部内容。
  • 如想了解你这次安装产生了哪些统计数据，或要求删除，请写信到下方地址。由于统计标识符是随机生成、不与你本人关联的，我们可能需要你游玩的大致日期和设备型号才能定位。
