SparkTread

Pixel-art tank battles for iPhone. Single player, offline, no ads.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: shawn.lee.im@gmail.com

The quickest route is the Feedback button in the game's Settings — it opens your mail app with the version, device and progress already filled in, which usually saves a round of questions. Nothing is sent until you send it.

Common questions

Does it need an internet connection?
No. The whole game runs on your device.
Is there an account or a purchase?
Neither. No sign-in, no in-app purchases, no advertising.
My progress disappeared.
Progress is saved on the device as each stage is cleared and is included in your device and iCloud backups. Deleting the app deletes it. If it vanished without deleting the app, please write — include the version shown in Settings.
Can I change the language?
Yes. The game follows your phone's language by default and can be set to English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어 or Español in Settings.
The controls are awkward for my left hand.
Settings has a mirror-controls switch, along with high contrast, a larger HUD, reduced motion and haptics.
iPad or Mac?
This version is built for iPhone. Other devices may come later.

常见问题

需要联网吗？
不需要。整个游戏都在你的设备上运行。
要账号或付费吗？
都不要。无需登录，没有内购，也没有广告。
我的进度不见了。
每通过一关进度都会保存在设备上，并包含在你的设备与 iCloud 备份中。删除应用即一并删除。如果没删应用却丢了进度，请写信告诉我，并附上「设置」里显示的版本号。
可以换语言吗？
可以。游戏默认跟随系统语言，也可在「设置」里选择 English、简体中文、繁體中文、日本語、한국어 或 Español。
操作对左手不太方便。
「设置」里有左右手互换开关，另有高对比度、放大 HUD、减弱动效和触感反馈。
支持 iPad 或 Mac 吗？
这一版为 iPhone 打造，其他设备后续再说。
